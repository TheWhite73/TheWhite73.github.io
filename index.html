Research interests

Probability and measure theory underneath, inverse problems and diffusion models on top, and one question that keeps pulling me back: what did the model actually learn?

  • Inverse problems

    Recovering a signal from indirect, noisy measurements; priors, ill-posedness and how many measurements recovery really needs.

  • Diffusion & generative models

    The theory behind score-based models: which distributions they can reach and at what sample cost.

  • Approximation theory of neural networks

    What universal approximation guarantees, and what it leaves unsaid about a trained network.

  • Probability & measure theory

    The language for saying precisely what it means to push a simple measure onto a complicated one.

Research experience

Covering numbers for Bayesian inverse problems

Jun – Sep 2026

Mitacs Globalink Research Intern, Simon Fraser University · advisor: Prof. Ben Adcock

  • Built PyTorch models to search for algorithms whose performance approaches theoretical covering-number bounds for a given distribution, evaluated on Fashion-MNIST and CelebA.
  • Modelled and generalised Bayesian priors so that the covering number of the underlying distribution becomes a usable measure of dataset complexity.
  • Took an open question with no predefined path, distilled the literature into a working framework, and isolated which assumptions actually drive the results.
  • Ongoing: extending the results to sparsity-promoting priors.
  • Bayesian inverse problems
  • Covering numbers
  • PyTorch
